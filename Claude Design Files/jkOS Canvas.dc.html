15 THE FENCE

Elevate inside these lines

Push craft on spacing, hierarchy, composition and atmosphere; keep the token names, the two-mode contract and the derivation chain intact.

DO
  • Extend the retro-hardware language as scarce punctuation.
  • Resolve every colour through the --hub-* / --color-* chain.
  • Reuse the shared classes; make both faces hold up.
  • Accent with wells (boundary) & badges (emphasis) — glow in CRT.
  • Fade colours with withAlpha().
DON'T
  • Rename any --hub-* token or hardcode a hex.
  • Use prefers-color-scheme — mode is data-mode.
  • Scatter hardware decoratively — an LED on everything means nothing is live.
  • Add an icon library or flatten the chrome toward SaaS.
  • Restate the derivation or re-set a CRT knob per app.
